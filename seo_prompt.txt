Important framing before you start

Google sitelinks are algorithmically generated — no developer or SEO technique can directly force them. They appear when a site has strong internal structure, clear hierarchy, sufficient authority, and (usually) get triggered on branded searches. Do NOT implement anything that resembles "fake" or "hidden" sitelinks-style markup — that reads as manipulative to Google. Instead, build the structural and authority signals that make sitelinks and rich results a natural outcome. Be explicit in your output about what's guaranteed (indexing, structured data eligibility) vs. probabilistic (rankings, sitelinks, review stars).
